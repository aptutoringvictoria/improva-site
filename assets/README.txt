Assets folder for the Improva website.

WHAT IS HERE
------------
  logo.png                 The app icon (512x512). Header brand mark, the icon
                           in the launch-notification box, and the Organization
                           logo in the JSON-LD.
  favicon.png              Favicon (64x64).
  apple-touch-icon.png     Apple touch icon (180x180), linked from every page.
  og-image.png             Social share image (1200x630): icon, tagline and the
                           Home screenshot, in the site's colours. Linked from
                           the Open Graph / Twitter tags.
  screenshot-1..5.webp     iPhone screenshots (660x1434) made from the Simulator
                           captures in screenshots/. The hero shows 1, the
                           first two features show 2, the gallery shows 3 to 5.
  app-store-badge-black.svg / app-store-badge-white.svg
                           Apple's official "Download on the App Store" badge,
                           downloaded from Apple's marketing tools. Not shown
                           yet: Apple requires it to link to the live listing.
                           The markup is commented out in index.html.
  fonts/                   Archivo SemiExpanded SemiBold and Bold (woff2,
                           subset to Latin) with its licence, Archivo-OFL.txt.
                           Converted from the app's FitnessLocker/Resources/Fonts.

Keep the filenames, or update the references in the HTML and style.css.
