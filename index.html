Everything you need to train and follow through, in one app.

Accountability, coaching, tracking, and nutrition, built to keep your focus where you want it.

Apps unlock when you show up at the gym.

Pick the apps that distract you. On a day you've scheduled a workout, they lock from the time you planned it; check in at your gym and start the session, and they open while you train. Skip it, and they stay locked until the end of that day. Plans change, so you can cancel a session ahead of time. Start a session and leave early, and they stay locked for longer. You choose how long. It uses Apple's Screen Time, and Improva never learns which apps you chose. Accountability is a Premium feature, free for 3 hours, once per iPhone.

Someone you trust can hold your key.

With Spare Key, you start a lock and give someone you trust the key: your apps stay locked until they send it to you. Your own emergency code ends it too. Spare Key is part of Premium; holding someone's spare key is free.

Accountability & app blocking

Lock the distracting apps you choose with Apple's Screen Time for the rest of the day when you skip the session you planned. Limited short "unlock bursts" and a private emergency code keep it firm but fair. There is one more lock: if next week isn't planned by the weekly deadline you set, apps lock until you add something.

Weekly planning

Lay out workouts, cardio, and focus blocks across the week in one place. Your plan drives your accountability and your streak, and with accountability on, Improva can remind you an hour before the weekly planning deadline you choose.

Workouts & guided timer

Build your own workouts with exercises, sets, reps, rest and supersets (or a quick time-based one), start from an evidence-based workout library, and turn your own workouts into a weekly split. A guided timer runs every set and rest, plays its cues over your music without stopping it, and logs what you lifted. If your machine is taken, it re-orders or swaps the exercise on the spot.

Personal trainer

Answer a few questions and get a multi-week progressive strength program. It applies double-progression as you log sets, schedules deloads, and lets you share a split or workout with a friend.

Running coach

Pick your goal (couch-to-5K through marathon, ultra, a time target, general fitness, or team pre-season) and get a full plan grounded in established running science, with paces, heart-rate zones, and safe progression.

Live cardio

Start a GPS session and see pace, distance, and heart rate (via a Bluetooth chest strap) with your route on a map. Structured intervals guide each phase with cues, plus a calorie estimate for the effort.

Nutrition & meal planner

Estimate your daily calorie, macro, and key-micronutrient targets, then log by search, barcode, or voice. A meal-plan generator fills a week from your diet and the ingredients you choose to hide, with a recipe library and batch-cook support. Ingredient data is derived automatically and may be incomplete, so always check the full ingredient list yourself and don't rely on it to avoid an allergy.

Progress tracking

Track personal records, estimate one-rep maxes from the sets you log, and chart your trends. Weights in kg or lb, your call.

Streaks

Complete the training you planned each week and your streak grows. It's a simple, honest nudge to keep showing up.

Apple Watch

Track cardio from your wrist, mirror the workout timer, and glance at a complication showing whether your apps are locked, your next session, and your streak.

Widgets & Siri

Home- and lock-screen widgets for your calorie ring, streak, and next session. Ask Siri to "start my workout in Improva" and Improva opens at today's session.

Backup & restore

Save your workouts, plans, nutrition, recipes, streak and settings to a file you own (keep it in Files or iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it to a new phone) and restore it on this phone or a new one. Your blocked-apps choice isn't included, because it's tied to the device, so you pick those apps again after restoring. Local-only shouldn't mean you can lose everything.
