Accountability

Optional

Apps unlock when you show up at the gym.

Pick the apps that distract you. On a day you've scheduled a workout, they lock from the time you planned it; check in at your gym and start the session, and they open while you train.

Skip it, and they stay locked until the end of that day. Plans change, so you can cancel a session ahead of time. Start a session and leave early, and they stay locked for longer. You choose how long.

It uses Apple's Screen Time, and Improva never learns which apps you chose. Accountability is a Premium feature, free for 3 hours, once per iPhone.

Spare Key

Optional

Someone you trust can hold your key.

With Spare Key, you start a lock and give someone you trust the key: your apps stay locked until they send it to you. Your own emergency code ends it too. Spare Key is part of Premium; holding someone's spare key is free.

Running coach and personal trainer

Real training, no shortcuts.

A running coach that builds a full plan from your goal, current fitness and schedule (from couch-to-5K to a marathon or ultra), and a personal trainer that generates progressive strength programs that adapt as you log your sets.

Nutrition

Micronutrients, not just macros

Estimate your daily calorie, macro and key-micronutrient targets, then log by search, barcode or voice and see the vitamins and minerals in what you log. These are estimates to guide you, not medical advice.

Meal planner

Meal plan to shopping list

The meal-plan generator fills a week from your diet and the ingredients you choose to hide, with a recipe library and batch-cook support. One shopping list adds up the week.

Ingredient data is derived automatically and may be incomplete, so always check the full ingredient list yourself and don't rely on it to avoid an allergy.

Also inside

Most of it stays free; accountability, the plan generators and cardio trends are Premium.

Live cardio
GPS pace, distance and your route, heart rate from a Bluetooth chest strap, and interval cues.
Guided timer
Runs every set and rest, logs what you lifted, and you can turn your own workouts into a weekly split.
Personal trainer
Multi-week strength programs that progress as you log your sets, with lighter weeks built in.
Weekly planning
Plan workouts, cardio and focus blocks by week. One more lock: if next week isn't planned by the weekly deadline you set, apps lock until you add something.
Bursts and the emergency code
A limited number of short unlock bursts, and a private emergency code for when you need out.
Progress
Personal records, estimated one-rep maxes and trends, in kg or lb.
Streaks
Complete the training you planned each week and your streak grows.
Apple Watch
Cardio from your wrist, the workout timer mirrored, and your lock status, next session and streak at a glance.
Widgets and Siri
Widgets for your calories, streak and next session. Ask Siri to "start my workout in Improva" and Improva opens at today's session.
Backup and restore
Save your plans, logs and settings to a file you own and restore it on this phone or a new one. Your blocked-apps choice isn't included, because it's tied to the device, so you pick those apps again after restoring.
Privacy
Your training, food and health data stay on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads. The privacy policy lists the few requests that do leave it.
