Everything inside

A closer look at what you get — every part shipped, all on your device.

Accountability & app blocking

Lock the distracting apps you choose with Apple's Screen Time until you complete the session you planned. Limited short "unlock bursts," a private emergency code, and a weekly planning lock keep it firm but fair.

Weekly planning

Lay out workouts, cardio, and focus blocks across the week in one place. Your plan drives your accountability and your streak, and each Sunday the app prompts you to plan the week ahead.

Workouts & guided timer

Build your own templates (simple or detailed with sets, reps, rest, and supersets) or start from an evidence-based library. A guided timer runs every set and rest with audio and haptic cues and logs what you lifted.

Personal trainer

Answer a few questions and get a multi-week progressive strength program. It applies double-progression as you log sets, schedules deloads, and lets you build or share your own split.

Running coach

Pick your goal — couch-to-5K through marathon, ultra, a time target, general fitness, or team pre-season — and get a full plan grounded in established running science, with paces, heart-rate zones, and safe progression.

Live cardio

Start a GPS session and see pace, distance, and heart rate (via Apple Watch or a Bluetooth strap) with your route on a map. Structured intervals guide each phase with cues, plus a calorie estimate for the effort.

Nutrition & meal planner

Estimate your daily calorie, macro, and key-micronutrient targets, then log by search, barcode, or voice. A meal-plan generator fills a week from your diet and allergen choices, with a recipe library and batch-cook support.

Progress tracking

Track personal records, estimate one-rep maxes from the sets you log, and chart your trends. Weights in kg or lb, your call.

Streaks

Complete the training you planned each week and your streak grows — a simple, honest nudge to keep showing up.

Apple Watch

Track cardio from your wrist, mirror the workout timer, and glance at a complication showing whether your apps are locked, your next session, and your streak.

Widgets, Siri & reminders

Home- and lock-screen widgets for your calorie ring, streak, and next session; "start my workout" with Siri; and timely reminders to plan and to train.

Backup & restore

Export all your data to a file you own — save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop to a new phone — and restore it anytime. Local-only shouldn't mean you can lose everything.
